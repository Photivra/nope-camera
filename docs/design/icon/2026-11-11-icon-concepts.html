nope. icon concepts

All four keep the current Photivra mark's language: the four corners of a landscape photo frame, the same 8-unit square-cap stroke, light (#EDEDEE) on Photivra's near-black (#111214). Teal (#1D9E75), nope.'s "verified" color, is used for one element only.

Concepts

Recommended

A. "n." in the frame

The Photivra "p" becomes a geometric "n" (same 17-unit arch and stroke), followed by a teal period: the "nope." wordmark reduced to one glyph. Clearly part of the Photivra family, clearly its own app.

B. Photivra "p."

The unchanged Photivra mark with a teal period. Strongest family tie, but at Home Screen size it can read as Photivra itself.

C. Empty frame, one dot

Just the photo frame and the teal period: nothing added to the picture. Most minimal; least explanatory.

D. Sensor pattern

A 2×2 Bayer cell (one teal "green" photosite) inside the frame: unprocessed RAW, literally. Most technical; busier when small.
