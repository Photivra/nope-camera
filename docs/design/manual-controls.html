nope. design: manual controls

The manual controls as the app ships them: the M pill, one readout row, a dial per control with − and + steppers and Auto, and Pro Tools. Drawn with the same camera screen as camera.html, in the v3 visual language (issue #68): filled white = selected, teal only for verified facts, amber only for warnings (always with a symbol and words), a dark backing for anything over the picture. Sizes are drawn at 0.75 × points; every target is at least 44 pt. Mockups only: where a drawing and the app differ, the app is right.

Interval shooting in full auto

Interval shooting also works with Manual Controls off, from the Drive menu in the quick-controls tray, with an Interval pill and the shutter starting and stopping the series. It is drawn with the camera screen: see camera.html.

Manual Controls settings

The app has no Manual Controls settings page. Settings › Camera has one switch, Manual Controls ("Shows manual controls on the camera screen. Off by default."); turning it off returns every control to Auto and turns bracketing off. Focus Peaking, Histogram and Live Camera Readout live in Settings › Viewfinder Overlays and in Pro Tools. See settings.html.

Rules behind it

  • Full auto is the default; manual controls appear only with the Settings switch, and the M pill turns them off again.
  • One readout row, fixed order; a control the lens can't set keeps its place ("Fixed" values or "n/a").
  • Filled white = the open readout and Auto while the camera sets the value. A/M letters carry the mode without color.
  • Every value has a button: − and + step it, Auto hands it back, All Auto resets everything. No gesture is required.
  • Anything over the picture (dial, hint, histogram) sits on a dark backing; amber only for the clipping warnings, always with a symbol and words.
  • Multi-frame tools never merge: each bracket frame and each interval photo is its own single-frame photo.
