The Manual Controls button in the lens row turns manual on and off in one tap, in full auto and in manual (issue 77). With manual on, a row of six readouts rests on the lower edge of the picture. Each shows A or M and its value; tapping one opens its dial above the row. More holds All Auto and Pro Tools. Pro Tools holds exposure mode, metering, the locks, bracketing and the live aids; Interval is only on the drive strip.
