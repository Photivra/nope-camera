Rules behind it

  • Colors have one meaning each: teal = verified fact, amber = warning, filled white = selected. Every state also has a symbol or words.
  • Success is quiet; anything that changes what the photo is, or a failure, stays visible until dealt with.
  • Every gesture (swipe, long press, double tap, drag) has a visible button that does the same.
  • Controls sit in the black bands; anything over the picture uses system materials with a dark backing (solid under Reduce Transparency / Increase Contrast).
  • Point budget (iPhone 16/17 size): top bar 44 · 4:3 picture · lens row 44 · manual readout 44 (when on) · shutter row 88. On 4.7-inch phones the dial and lens row overlay the bottom of the picture.
  • System button styles and SF Symbols; right-to-left mirrors the bottom row; banners may wrap to two lines for longer languages.
