Settings tree (proposal)

Proposal for review, 2026-11-17. Not decided, and not yet drawn as screens. The reasons, the inventory and the decisions are in SETTINGS-PLAN.md. The current design is in settings.html.

Settings opens from Q on iPhone (two taps from the camera) and from its own button on iPad and the iPhone Duo inner display (one tap). A search field heads the list. Each indent is one level; nothing is more than two levels below Settings.
