App icon

Design version: 1.0.0, direction B · Reach (2026-11-17). Planned design for release 1.0.0; the app is built to match it. Includes the Settings structure and the device coverage accepted on 2026-11-17 (SETTINGS-PLAN.md).

The icon is the “n.” in the Photivra photo frame (concept A, kept by the owner): four corners of a landscape frame, an 8-unit square-cap stroke, light on near-black. The color of the period was the open decision; it is teal. Each option is shown as the default, dark and light icons, and at 60 pt. The final icon is a brand asset; see TRADEMARKS.md. The shapes are drawn from scratch and use no font.

Decided (2026-11-17): the period is teal (option 1). Teal is the one accent in the app and means a verified fact; the period is the same teal as the RAW seal, so the icon and the first thing on the camera screen agree. It also keeps the icon distinct from the Photivra mark at small sizes. The icon cannot follow system colors, so it uses a fixed value: #40C8E0, the fact color in tokens.md.

The icon is built as an Icon Composer file with separate layers: App/NopeApp/AppIcon.icon for iPhone and iPad (default, dark and tinted appearances) and App/NopeWatch/AppIcon.icon for Apple Watch (the mark at 80% so the frame clears the circle). The light-ground variant is not used.

1 · Teal period (chosen)

#40C8E0 on the near-black ground, 9.4:1; on the black dark-appearance ground, 10.6:1. The light-ground icon (third, with #0e6f66) is not shipped.

2 · Period in the stroke color

One color only. The quietest, but at 60 pt it is close to the Photivra mark, and the “nope.” full stop loses its emphasis.

3 · Red period

The system red (#ff453a; #d70015 on the light ground). Distinct, but red reads as recording or an error, and in the app red belongs to night mode only.

Variants to produce

  • Default: ground #111214, strokes #EDEDEE, period #40C8E0.
  • Dark: black ground; strokes and period unchanged.
  • Tinted: one layer set, which iOS tints; the period stays as a shape.
  • Light (optional, not used): ground #F4F4F2, strokes #111214.
  • Layers: background, frame, “n”, period.
