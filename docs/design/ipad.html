A full-size iPad window uses the column layout in both orientations: the picture with a trailing column in reach of the right hand, holding Settings, the lenses, Manual Controls, Q, the shutter, Last Photo and Switch Camera. The drive strip, cards, dials and readouts rest on the lower edge of the picture, 480 pt wide at most. Drawn at 1024 × 768 pt as an example size. The rule for screens not drawn here is in layouts.html.
