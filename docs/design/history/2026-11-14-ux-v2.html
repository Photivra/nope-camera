nope. — clean camera layout (v2)

Proposal following Apple's Human Interface Guidelines: the picture first, controls only where the thumb expects them, short transient messages instead of persistent text, system materials and SF Symbols. Mockups only; nothing is built yet.

What changes from today

  • Full-width viewfinder under the status bar (today: inset box with margins)
  • Small round buttons on system material, SF Symbols (today: large gear circle)
  • Format badge becomes a compact chip; "Single frame" moves to the photo details (less text)
  • Lens picker as a compact pill over the picture; front camera is its own flip button
  • Status messages are short toasts that fade, announced to VoiceOver (today: a line of text that stays)
  • Thumbnail bottom-left, shutter center, camera flip bottom-right (platform convention)
  • Manual: one readout row plus a dial, not a grid of tiles
  • Settings: short grouped list; explanations move a level down
  • Landscape: fixed layout, rotating icons
  • Haptic on shutter, lens change and lock; Dynamic Type and VoiceOver kept for every control
