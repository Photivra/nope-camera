Clean view: the bars, the swipe area and the guide page (issues 166, 174, 175)

In clean view the status bar and the Home indicator hide. A vertical swipe in the empty area below the picture toggles clean view, as a swipe on the picture does. The guide gains a page about it.

  • What iOS always shows stays: the Dynamic Island and the camera-in-use dot. The system's swipes keep working.
  • A swipe that starts on the shutter, Q, Last Photo or any other control does what that control does today.
  • The swipe is off in the scrolling layout at the largest text sizes, as it is on the picture, and it is never on Q.
  • The guide's sentence about the empty space is included only if issue 166 ships in the same release.

Proposed 2026-11-28 for release 1.1.0. A sketch of layout, states and wording, not a pixel-exact screen. The specification is in release-1.1.md; the plan is in docs/plans/1.1.0.md. Wording is a draft until the Honesty review.
