Light Meter (issue 184)

A reflected-light meter built on the reading the camera's own exposure meter already gives. Built only if the research step is a go (plan, R16).

  • It reads the camera's exposure meter. It adds no image processing, saves nothing and writes nothing to a photo.
  • Whole Frame, Center and Spot are the Metering choices the app already has. Spot is the area iOS meters around the point, not a one-degree spot.
  • Accuracy is stated only as measured in R16, for the devices tested. Until then the sheet says it is not calibrated against a reference.
  • A Q tile is not part of this design. Adding one would change Q and needs owner sign-off before build.

Proposed 2026-11-28 for release 1.1.0. A sketch of layout, states and wording, not a pixel-exact screen. The specification is in release-1.1.md; the plan is in docs/plans/1.1.0.md. Wording is a draft until the Honesty review.
