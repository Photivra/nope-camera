Shooting Modes (issue 172)

A Settings page that chooses which shooting modes appear in the camera's row. All are on by default.

  • Hiding the current mode switches the camera to Single. A series already running continues to its end.
  • A mode chosen from somewhere else while it is hidden (a preset, Shortcuts) shows in the row while it is the current mode.
  • The choice is made in Settings only. The row cannot be edited on the camera screen, and Q is not changed.

Proposed 2026-11-28 for release 1.1.0. A sketch of layout, states and wording, not a pixel-exact screen. The specification is in release-1.1.md; the plan is in docs/plans/1.1.0.md. Wording is a draft until the Honesty review.
