Apple Watch: one status at a time, and Start Burst (issues 170, 171, 181)

Design comps for the owner's approval before the build (issue 171). One current status; at most one earlier result, dated; a disabled button that looks unavailable and says what to do; Start Burst and Stop in Burst.

  • The status line is the current state only. The last result never shows as a second status line.
  • The chip shows a symbol and the time since the photo (Just now, 1 min, 2 min …). It is removed after 30 minutes and when the watch app starts again.
  • Teal only for a verified fact (saved and signed). Amber only for warnings, always with a symbol and words. No yellow.
  • The watch says "the app" for Photivra Nope Camera: the watch app is its remote, and the sentences stay short (issue 183).
  • Always On shows the status line and the button's outline, as today; the count of a running burst is not updated while the wrist is down.

Proposed 2026-11-28 for release 1.1.0. A sketch of layout, states and wording, not a pixel-exact screen. The specification is in release-1.1.md; the plan is in docs/plans/1.1.0.md. Wording is a draft until the Honesty review.
