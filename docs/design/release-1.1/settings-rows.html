Settings rows: one alignment rule, and Show Steady Hint (issues 173, 178)

Every Settings row with a value or a picker follows one rule. Show Steady Hint is a new switch in the Screen section.

  • A value and its chevron share the first baseline of the title; the explanation runs under the title at full width.
  • A picker inside a row becomes a menu on the trailing side, so its value lines up with the other values.
  • The Burst Limit row is not drawn: issue 180 removes the setting.

Proposed 2026-11-28 for release 1.1.0. A sketch of layout, states and wording, not a pixel-exact screen. The specification is in release-1.1.md; the plan is in docs/plans/1.1.0.md. Wording is a draft until the Honesty review.
