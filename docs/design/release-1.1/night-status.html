Night Mode: the app's own time and battery (issue 176)

While Night Mode is on, the camera screen and the Last Photo viewer hide the iOS status bar and show the time and the battery in Night Mode's red, where the status bar showed them.

  • The time follows the person's 12- or 24-hour setting and changes on the minute. The battery is the level iOS reports, in steps of 5%, and whether it is charging.
  • No signal strength: an app cannot read it.
  • VoiceOver reads one element: "10:42 PM, battery 45 percent, charging".
  • The indicator sits in the band the status bar leaves, beside the Dynamic Island. It covers no part of the picture and no warning, and the layout does not move.
  • Without Night Mode nothing is added. In clean view without Night Mode the bar is hidden and no time shows (issue 175).
