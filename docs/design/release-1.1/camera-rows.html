Camera rows: gaps and the edge fade (issue 169)

The drive strip and the manual readouts. No two controls touch; a row that does not fit scrolls, clips at its edge and fades toward the hidden content.

  • The gap between two drive words is 8 pt (NopeSpace.xs) in the fitted row and in the scrolling row, at every text size.
  • The same rule applies in Night Mode, right to left (mirrored), sideways and on iPad.
  • Q, the quick menu, is not changed.

Proposed 2026-11-28 for release 1.1.0. A sketch of layout, states and wording, not a pixel-exact screen. The specification is in release-1.1.md; the plan is in docs/plans/1.1.0.md. Wording is a draft until the Honesty review.
