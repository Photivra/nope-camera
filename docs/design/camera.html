nope. design: camera

The camera screen and everything that opens from it, drawn from the 0.3.0 app (Sources/NopeUI, App/) in the v3 visual language (issue #68). It replaces ux-v3.html as the camera design. Sizes are drawn at 0.75 × points; every target is at least 44 pt. Mockups only: where a drawing and the app differ, the app is right.

Design version: 0.3.0 (2026-11-16) · Set: camera.html · settings.html (Settings and system screens) · manual-controls.html (manual dials and Pro Tools) · icon.html (app icon) · README.md

Camera screen

Full auto shows six things: the format chip, Settings, the lens row with ⋯, the shutter, the camera flip and, after the first photo, the thumbnail. Everything else appears only when it isn't at its default.

Quick-controls tray

⋯ at the end of the lens row turns into a filled ✕ and swaps the lenses for one scrolling row. Switches fill white when on; menus are native menus; the tray closes on an item's action, the shutter, or a tap on the picture.

Flash and Light

Flash is a menu of the modes the lens supports; Light is the torch, a steady light that stays on while photos are taken.

Drive

Single, timer, burst and interval. Bracketing is in Pro Tools (manual-controls.html). While a countdown, burst or series runs, the shutter becomes its Stop button and VoiceOver says so.

The thumbnail and "What's in This Photo"

Only photos taken since nope. opened: nope. has add-only access to the photo library and keeps these details in memory only.

Save feedback

Success is quiet. A failure is a banner that stays until it is dismissed or the next photo saves, takes VoiceOver focus, and offers at most one action.

Status chips and hints

Amber chips sit at the picture's top edge in this order: Not Signed, storage, battery, Light Unavailable, Location On. Each opens its explanation; VoiceOver reads it as the chip's value. Hints are white on a dark backing: advice, not warnings.

Focus aids and overlays

Drawn on the picture, never saved in the photo. Grid, level, peaking and the readout are switched in the tray or in Viewfinder Overlays (settings.html).

Compare

nope.'s photo next to a second photo with the phone's full processing. The second photo is never saved.

Astro

A helper for night sky photos: a series of single RAW frames, each saved as its own photo, for stacking in other software. Turning Astro on also turns Night Mode on.

Presets

Named sets of every setting: photos, privacy, camera, and the lens, exposure and drive mode. The same list is in Settings.

Night mode

Red on black on every screen nope. draws, including sheets. Teal and amber also become red, so symbols and words carry the meaning. nope. never changes the screen's brightness.

Outside the app

The Live Activity is off by default (Settings › Live Activity); it shows counts and status only, updated on the iPhone and never sent anywhere.

Rules behind it

  • Colors have one meaning each: teal = verified fact, amber = warning, filled white = selected. No yellow. Every state also has a symbol or words; in night mode teal and amber become red and the symbols carry the meaning.
  • Success is quiet; anything that changes what the photo is, or a failure, stays visible until dealt with.
  • Every gesture has a visible button: Hide/Show Controls for the swipe, Lock for the long press, −/+ and Reset or Auto on every dial, Cancel Timer and Stop for running drive modes.
  • Controls sit in the black bands; anything over the picture has a dark backing (90% black over a material, solid with Reduce Transparency or Increase Contrast).
  • Point budget (iPhone 16/17 size): top bar 44 · 4:3 picture · lens row 44 · manual readout when on · shutter row 88. Targets at least 44 × 44 with 8 between; shutter 76; thumbnail and flip 52.
  • Sideways iPhone: the layout stays put, each icon and text block turns upright. iPad: trailing column, real rotation. Right-to-left mirrors rows, never the picture.
