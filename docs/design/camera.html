The camera screen in direction B. Everything used while shooting is in the lower third, in reach of a thumb: the lens row, the drive strip, and the shutter row with Last Photo and Q. The top line is status. Controls stay off the picture unless they are needed at that moment; anything that does sit on the picture has a dark backing and rests on an edge.
