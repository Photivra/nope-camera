Drive modes

Design version: 1.0.0, direction B · Reach (2026-11-17). Planned design for release 1.0.0; the app is built to match it. Includes the Settings structure and the device coverage accepted on 2026-11-17 (SETTINGS-PLAN.md).

The five drive modes exclude one another, so they are one strip of words above the shutter. A use case is one tap away and its name is always on screen. The selected mode’s card rests on the lower edge of the picture: idle, it names what the shutter will do; running, it shows progress and the stop button, and the other words dim.

  • Timer shows its delay on the shutter, so it needs no card.
  • Bracketing stays in Pro Tools, with the other manual tools.
  • Astro still turns on Manual Controls and night mode, as the app does today; turning Manual Controls off turns Astro off.
  • While a series runs there is nothing to adjust, so the manual readouts step back.
