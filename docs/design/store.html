App Store page

Layout mockups for the App Store screenshots of release 1.0.0 (2026-11-19). They show the layout, the captions and the order; they are not the images that are uploaded.

The plan, the storyboards and the rule for each image are in docs/store/STORE-PAGE-PLAN.md, sections 3 to 6. Each image is a black ground with a caption in Inter and the captured screen under it, with no device frame. The first image of the iPhone and iPad sets carries the wordmark lockup. Apple Watch images are the bare screen.

  • The screen inside each mockup is an existing design figure, not a capture. The final images use captures of the built app, so wording and details follow the build.
  • A dashed outline with the words “Placeholder picture” marks every place where a real picture goes: a capture from the iPhone, a photo taken with the app, or a system screen. The drawn landscape under it is the design set’s placeholder and never appears on the store page.
  • An image is uploaded only when the checks named for it in the plan are recorded. iPhone images 6, 7, 9 and 10 wait for theirs.
  • Captions are set in Inter (SIL Open Font License). The PNG files are rendered with it; the HTML pages name it and fall back to another sans serif where it isn’t installed.
