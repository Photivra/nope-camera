A small site for people who use Photivra Nope Camera: a landing page, the privacy policy, support and accessibility. It keeps the app’s rules: no cookies, no analytics, no JavaScript, and no request to any other site. The structure, the draft copy, the build specification, the checks and the decisions are in nope-site.md.

These figures are drawings of the pages, not the site’s code. Their text is set in Inter (SIL Open Font License), embedded in each file. Screenshots and the App Store badge are labeled placeholders: the real ones are captures of the app and Apple’s own artwork.
