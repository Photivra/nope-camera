The iPhone Duo has two displays, and the app treats each as a window. Sizes are those measured on the iPhone Duo Simulator and used by the layout tests: the outer display is 466 × 678 pt with the status bar in a trailing strip, 382 × 644 pt for the app; the inner display is 951 × 669 pt held like a book (867 × 635 pt for the app) and 669 × 951 pt turned (669 × 835 pt).

  • Outer display: compact width, so the iPhone design. The 4:3 picture and the rows do not both fit in 644 pt, so the lens row and the drive strip rest on the picture’s lower edge, as on the 4.7-inch iPhone. Settings opens from Q as a sheet. The outer display stays portrait; with the phone on its side, symbols and words turn upright.
  • Inner display: regular in both size classes, so the iPad design: the column layout, Settings as a popover from its own button, centered sheets. It rotates for real, like an iPad.
  • Folding and unfolding: whatever is open stays open and keeps its place. Only the layout changes. A presentation keeps the style it opened with.
  • Half-open: no special layout in 1.0.0.
  • Every pair below is expected behavior read from the code. A fold cannot be driven by a UI test, so each is checked by hand on the Simulator and on a device (docs/TESTING.md, iPhone Duo).

The rule for screens not drawn here is in layouts.html: iPhone drawings apply to the outer display and iPad drawings to the inner display.
