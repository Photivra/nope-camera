Design version: 1.0.0, direction B · Reach (2026-11-17). Planned design for release 1.0.0; the app is built to match it. Includes the Settings structure and the device coverage accepted on 2026-11-17 (SETTINGS-PLAN.md), and the Last Photo viewer and the Photo Details redesign of 2026-11-18.

Owner decision, 2026-11-18: a tap on Last Photo shows the photo just taken, full screen inside nope., in place of opening the Photos app. iOS cannot open Photos at one photo, and nope. has add-only access to Photos, so it cannot read a photo back. The viewer shows what nope. still holds: a screen-size preview of each photo taken since the app opened, in memory only, never on disk, released when the app leaves the screen (AGENTS.md, Photos never linger). It replaces the Last Photo menu. The interaction details are in ACCESSIBILITY.md (Last Photo viewer) and the build steps in BUILD-SPEC.md, section 3.14.

  • The photo is the screen. It fits the space the rows leave and nothing covers it; a tap leaves it alone on black.
  • The camera’s places are kept. Status is the top line. The actions are in reach, where the shutter row is, and Close is on the same side as Last Photo, near where the same thumb opened the viewer. In release 1.0 the two don’t line up exactly: Close is at the leading edge of a fixed 88-point row, about 33 to 49 points nearer the edge than Last Photo on iPhone, and up to 13.5 points lower on the large iPhones (final review of 1.0.0, from the layout code). Lining them up is a later release’s choice. Wider windows keep the camera’s trailing column.
  • Honest about what is shown. The status line states read-back facts only: formats, Processed, Signed or Not signed. A zoomed photo says it is a preview. When only the thumbnail is left, the viewer shows it small and says why.
  • Two actions, both existing: Photo Details (What’s in This Photo) and Open in Photos. Nothing is shared, edited or deleted here.
  • Full screen on every device, as Compare is. On iPad a sheet would letterbox the photo for no gain.
