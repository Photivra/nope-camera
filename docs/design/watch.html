The watch app is one screen: the status, the last result, the shutter and the delay. It takes single photos and the self-timer only. Drawn at 46 mm (208 × 248 pt) unless a size is named. Every sentence is the app’s existing string.

What the watch app does not have

  • No settings. Its one choice is Delay (Off, 3 seconds, 10 seconds), kept only while the watch app runs. It is separate from the phone’s self-timer and is not mirrored either way.
  • No phone setting is shown or changed on the watch. One is reflected: the result reads Saved, not signed or Saved and signed according to Sign Each Photo on the phone.
  • No viewfinder, no lens choice, no drive modes, no manual controls and no Q. A burst, an interval series or Astro cannot be started from the watch; while the phone is busy with one, the watch answers The camera was busy.
  • No night mode on the watch: the watch app does not turn red when Night Mode is on. No complication and no Smart Stack widget.
  • No special Always On drawing: the system dims the app.

These are statements of what exists in 1.0.0, not gaps in the drawings. None is added in 1.0.0; a stored Delay, or night mode on the watch, would be new capabilities.
