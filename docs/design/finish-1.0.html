The last designs for 1.0.0

Proposed 2026-11-21 for release 1.0.0 and built in 1.0.0 with the owner’s decisions of 2026-11-21. The specification is finish-1.0.md; the plan and the decisions for the owner are in docs/plans/1.0.0-finish.md. All wording is a draft until the Honesty review. Space between the control rows (issue 116)

The picture keeps its size. The spare height below it is shared between the gap under the lens row and the shutter row, instead of all going to the shutter row. The marks at the right edge of each figure give the space between the shapes, in points; they are not part of the screen. Specification.

The how-to-use guide (issue 117)

A small card on the camera at first launch, a guide of seven short pages, and the rows that reopen it. Specification.

The summary card of What’s in This Photo (issue 118)

One group in the style of the groups under it, with a row for each fact and one symbol column. The card as built is in the Photo Details figures. Specification.

Dim After (issue 119)

A choice under Keep Screen On, with Never, and the note that gives its cost. Specification.

Burst Limit (issue 121)

The setting, and the burst card with a limit set. The burst card without a limit is in Burst. Specification.

Interval, start to start (issue 123)

The sentence in Interval Shooting, and the card when photos take longer than the interval. The self-timer’s deadline (issue 122) changes nothing that can be drawn. Specification.
