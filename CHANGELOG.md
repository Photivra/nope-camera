## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-18

### Added
- Interval shooting can stop after a time instead of a number of photos (owner decision, 2026-11-18). Interval Settings has Stop After (a picker on its own page, as in Astro Settings; it takes the place of the sheet's repeated "Interval Shooting" title row): Number of Photos (as before, the default) or Time, with Hours and Minutes (1 minute to 24 hours) and the most photos the series can take ("At most 360"); Start is disabled, and the sheet and the card say "Choose an interval shorter than the time.", while the interval isn't shorter than the time. A timed series takes a photo every interval until the time is up; its end is fixed on the wall clock when it starts, and the time keeps running while the series is paused, so a series paused past its end ends when the camera is back, without another photo. A stop, a photo that can't be taken or saved, and a camera that stops still end it early. The idle card says "Every 10 seconds, for 1 hour, 30 minutes"; the running card "12 photos taken · 1 hr, 12 min left" (the units in words for VoiceOver) with a progress bar by time and the next photo's time, and while paused when it ends. The Live Activity counts the time left down and fills its bar by time. Presets keep the choice and the time; older presets read as Number of Photos. Start Interval Shooting has an optional Duration in Minutes (it takes priority over Number of Photos). `NopeCore`: `IntervalSettings.StopAfter`, `duration`, `maximumPhotoCount`, `IntervalProgress.timeSpan`, `ManualControlsModel.lastIntervalEnd`, `IntervalBlocker.intervalNotShorterThanTime`, `ShortcutOutcome.intervalNotShorterThanTime`, `IntervalShortcut.durationSeconds`, `LiveActivityContent.seriesTime`, `CameraSetup.intervalStopAfter` and `intervalDuration`; `CameraModel(currentDate:)` reads the wall clock. Unit tests (`IntervalTimeTests`, `IntervalTimeCardTests`) and UI tests with accessibility audits at the default and largest text sizes, right to left and in night mode (`IntervalTimeUITests.swift`).
- The app icon (issue #74, `docs/design/BUILD-SPEC.md` section 6, item 11): the "n." in the photo frame with a teal period (owner decision, 2026-11-17), as Icon Composer files with separate frame, "n" and period layers drawn as vector paths from `docs/design/icon.html` (no font). `App/NopeApp/AppIcon.icon` gives iPhone and iPad the default, dark and tinted appearances; `App/NopeWatch/AppIcon.icon` is the Apple Watch icon, with the mark at 80% so the frame stays inside the circle. The period is `#40C8E0`, the `fact` color in `docs/design/tokens.md`. The app and Apple Watch targets set `ASSETCATALOG_COMPILER_APPICON_NAME = AppIcon`; the extensions use the app's icon. `docs/design/icon.html` shows the choice.
- Design tokens and button styles for the 1.0.0 interface (issue #74, `docs/design/BUILD-SPEC.md`, chunk 0). `NopeUI`: `DesignTokens.swift` with `NopeSpace`, `NopeRadius`, `NopeSize`, `NopeFont` (Dynamic Type text styles, and `nopeCountdownFont()` for the scaled countdown), `NopeColor`, `NopeMaterial` (`nopeBackground(_:in:)`, solid black with Reduce Transparency or Increase Contrast), `NopeMotion` (with the Reduce Motion forms and the pressed and disabled states), `NopeHaptic` and `nopeHitTarget()`, named one to one with `docs/design/tokens.md`; `NopeButtonStyles.swift` with `NopeIconButtonStyle`, `NopeCapsuleStyle` (selectable and destructive), `NopeTileStyle` and `NopeTileLabel`, and `NopePillStyle`, each with normal, pressed, selected, disabled, night-mode and Increase Contrast states and a 44-point target. `CameraStyle`, `DarkBacking`, `darkBacking()` and `SelectableCapsuleStyle` now take their values from the tokens and are kept, documented as deprecated, until the views move to the new types; the default chip and backing corner radius is 12 points (was 10). No view uses the new types yet, so the camera looks as before apart from that radius. Unit tests check every token's name and value against `tokens.md` and the contrast of the color pairs. Debug builds: `-NopeButtonGallery` (with `-NopeButtonGallery.night YES` for night mode) shows every button type and state, with a UI test that audits it at the default and largest text sizes, right to left and in night mode.
- Strings for the 1.0.0 interface (issue #74, `docs/design/BUILD-SPEC.md` section 4, chunk S), added to the string catalogs in all eleven languages as AI translations marked "needs review", so the interface chunks only reference keys: the drive-strip words, the Quick Menu, the Manual Controls button words, the mode-card lines, the Processed marker and processing note, the Settings names and one-sentence explanations without default notes, the Settings search headers, pointers, page paths and keywords, the preset menu and delete confirmation, and the Honesty entries Signing Is Your Choice and Lenses Without RAW, with the Location and Faces and Autofocus entries reworded for the renamed settings. - Q, the quick menu (issue #74, `docs/design/BUILD-SPEC.md`, 3.6, chunk B). `NopeUI`: `QuickPanel.swift` with `QuickPanel`, a panel of tiles on the dark backing that replaces the sideways-scrolling quick-controls tray, and `QuickMenuList`, the same tiles as a full-height list with Done at accessibility text sizes.
